## Financial investigation, detection of irregularities and technical evidence

At CENIPSA S.A.S. we combine accounting and financial science with legal and criminalistic investigation to examine financial records, transactions and books of account. Our forensic auditors and expert accountants analyze complex financial structures to identify, document and prove asset irregularities or economic crimes.

We prepare accounting expert reports and technical reports with a strict chain of custody, designed to support legal action before civil, criminal and labor courts or arbitration tribunals.

## Our forensic audit services

- **Financial fraud investigation and detection:** identification of embezzlement, misappropriation, falsification of accounting records, over-invoicing and conflicts of interest in corporate or personal settings.
- **Accounting analysis and reconstruction of financial flows:** methodical inspection and reverse engineering of financial statements, bank movements and balance sheets to trace the origin, destination and traceability of funds.
- **Party-appointed accounting expert reports:** independent, technically grounded expert reports to quantify damages, consequential losses, lost profits or contractual imbalances.
- **Forensic audit in corporate and family disputes:** technical evaluation of financial statements and asset inventories in the dissolution of companies, divorces, liquidation of marital property and disputes between partners.
