At CENIPSA S.A.S. we carry out location and demographic research to meet legal, procedural and family needs. We combine field investigation and consultation of public sources and official databases with the work of lawyers and private investigators, ensuring accurate, legally valid information for court notifications and legal formalities.

## Our Services for Locating People

### 1. Locating Parties and Debtors for Service of Process

- **Locating defendants and parties to litigation:** finding the home or workplace of people required in civil, labor, commercial or family proceedings so that orders and claims can be effectively served.
- **Locating defaulting debtors:** lawful demographic research to locate obligors in enforcement proceedings, debt collection or fulfillment of obligations.

### 2. Searching for and Identifying Missing Heirs and Relatives

- **Locating heirs and beneficiaries:** documentary and field searches for heirs or legatees whose whereabouts are unknown, so they can be joined to notarial or judicial probate proceedings.
- **Identifying relatives:** technical tracing of background and family lines to restore family contact or recover property rights.

### 3. Investigation in Paternity and Civil-Status Proceedings

- **Locating parties for paternity or maternity proceedings:** finding alleged parents or interested parties so they can be formally joined to recognition or challenge proceedings.
- **Documentary reconstruction of parentage:** support in obtaining civil registry records and official documentation in Colombia or abroad.
