## Protecting digital assets, incident response and electronic evidence

At CENIPSA S.A.S. we offer comprehensive solutions for managing cyber risk and protecting the strategic information of individuals and companies. We combine legal consulting in IT law with systems engineering and digital forensics, ensuring preventive vulnerability assessment and the lawful collection of digital evidence when incidents occur.

All our work follows international information-security protocols and strictly complies with Colombian cybercrime law and personal data protection regulations.

## Our cybersecurity and digital forensics services

- **Vulnerability assessment and digital risk analysis:** technical evaluation and security audits of networks, servers, operating systems and corporate web platforms to identify security gaps and prevent unauthorized access.
- **Digital forensics and electronic evidence acquisition:** extraction, preservation and technical analysis of data on computers, mobile devices, email and servers under digital chain-of-custody standards, so the evidence holds up in proceedings.
- **Security incident management and response:** immediate support and technical containment of confidential data leaks, availability attacks, identity theft or network intrusions.
- **Digital compliance and data protection:** legal and technical advice on implementing personal data processing policies, information security and the protection of trade secrets.
