CENIPSA S.A.S. is a Colombian company with extensive experience in private investigation and law, based in Bogotá. We provide specialized investigative and judicial guidance so that individuals and organizations can overcome the consequences of crimes and civil liabilities. Our core purpose is to help build justice and restore the rights of those who have been affected by different forms of wrongdoing.

Our interdisciplinary team brings together lawyers, judicial investigators, social scientists, engineers and forensic experts, backed by clear service guarantees for every client. Explore our [services](/en/services/) or [contact us](/en/contact/) to discuss your case in confidence.

## Our mission

At CENIPSA S.A.S. we contribute to the restoration of violated rights. We work with an investigative, social and legal approach that allows us to gather evidence and clarify the facts under investigation in pursuit of the truth. Our team has the knowledge, expertise and strategic capacity to advise you on both [personal](/en/personal-investigation/) and [corporate](/en/corporate-investigation/) matters.

## Our vision

By 2030, CENIPSA S.A.S. will be recognized throughout Colombia for its contribution to truth and justice through judicial investigation, strengthening national and international institutional alliances as a consultative ally in the defense of human rights, anti-corruption and the reduction of inequality.

## Our pillars

The right combination of experience and technology.

### Confidentiality

Every case is handled with absolute discretion and full protection of our clients' information.

### Legal rigor

Evidence gathered on solid legal grounds, admissible and useful both inside and outside the courtroom — from [criminalistics](/en/criminalistics/) to [polygraph examinations](/en/polygraph/).

### Truth

An investigative, social and legal approach focused on clarifying the facts of each case.

### Competence

An interdisciplinary team with proven theoretical, methodological, practical and ethical expertise, complemented by [legal advice](/en/legal-advice/).

## A seal of quality: affiliated with the Bogotá Chamber of Commerce

CENIPSA belongs to the Bogotá Chamber of Commerce's Circle of Affiliates, a credential granted to companies with a reliable, impeccable track record and more than 10 years of business activity.
